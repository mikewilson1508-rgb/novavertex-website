Talent coordination

A more structured path from vacancy to shortlist.

Recruitment coordination support that helps hiring teams organize role requirements, candidate pipelines and interview logistics.

How we can support you

We can support agreed stages of the recruitment workflow, from role intake to interview coordination, with clear updates so hiring managers know what is moving and what needs attention.

Role intake and planning

Create a shared understanding of the vacancy before sourcing begins.

  • ✓ Role brief and must-have criteria capture
  • ✓ Job-description formatting and posting coordination
  • ✓ Target profile and screening checklist preparation

Candidate sourcing and screening support

Organize candidate discovery and initial review against agreed criteria.

  • ✓ Sourcing support across agreed channels
  • ✓ CV/resume review against stated requirements
  • ✓ Shortlist preparation and candidate status tracking

Interview coordination

Help keep communication and scheduling organized across stakeholders.

  • ✓ Interview scheduling and reminders
  • ✓ Candidate and hiring-manager coordination
  • ✓ Feedback collection and pipeline updates

Offer and onboarding coordination

Support the handoff once a preferred candidate is selected.

  • ✓ Offer-stage status coordination
  • ✓ Document checklist and onboarding follow-up
  • ✓ Recruitment funnel and time-to-stage reporting

Engagement and boundaries

Final hiring decisions, employment-law advice, right-to-work determinations and regulated background checks remain the responsibility of the client and authorized specialists. Candidate information should be handled in line with applicable privacy and employment requirements.
