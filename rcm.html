Healthcare operations

Keep billing workflows visible, organized and moving.

Administrative RCM support for healthcare organizations that want consistent claim workflows, clear exception tracking and dependable follow-up.

How we can support you

We help structure the operational work around the claim lifecycle. Scope can be tailored to your practice systems, payer mix, internal controls, documentation standards and authorized access.

Front-end and charge workflows

Help reduce avoidable workflow gaps before claims move through the billing process.

  • ✓ Eligibility and benefits verification support
  • ✓ Charge-entry and claim-preparation checks
  • ✓ Missing-information and authorization follow-up

Claims, denials and accounts receivable

Maintain visibility into submitted claims, payer responses, aging and next actions.

  • ✓ Claim status follow-up and workqueue management
  • ✓ Denial categorization and appeal-packet preparation
  • ✓ Aging reports, payer trends and escalation lists

ERA/EOB and payment workflows

Support review of remittance information and posting-related exceptions.

  • ✓ ERA/EOB review and payment-posting support
  • ✓ Unapplied or partially applied payment exception lists
  • ✓ Reconciliation and follow-up reporting

Quality and reporting

Use repeatable checks to make work easier to monitor and audit.

  • ✓ Daily or weekly workqueue summaries
  • ✓ Claim and denial trend reports
  • ✓ Documented escalation and quality review steps

Engagement and boundaries

All work must follow applicable privacy, security, payer, contract and organizational requirements. Access should be limited to authorized users. Clinical decisions, coding determinations and payer representations must remain with appropriately qualified personnel as required. Do not submit patient data through the public website contact form.
