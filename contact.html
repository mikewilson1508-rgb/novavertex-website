Yes. The engagement can be scoped around one service or a combination, subject to capacity, access and the agreed responsibilities.

Do you work with existing software and processes?

The intended approach is to understand the systems and controls already in place, then agree the workflow, permissions and handoffs before work starts.

Is the website enquiry form live?

No. This is a design preview. A form service or backend must be connected and tested before launch.

Can you provide regulated tax, audit or clinical services?

Services requiring a professional license, certification or formal sign-off must be performed by appropriately authorized professionals. Scope should be confirmed for each engagement.
